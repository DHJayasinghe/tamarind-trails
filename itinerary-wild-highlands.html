B — Wildlife & Coast

The Wild Highlands
& East Coast Loop

Peak Performance, Coastal Calm

Duration 7 Days / 6 Nights Best For Ages 12–60 · Repeat Visitors · Nature Enthusiasts Tiers Essential · Boutique · Signature
Route — Airport-Colombo → Belihul Oya → Haputale → Ella → Kumana National Park → Tangalle → Airport-Colombo

This curated loop is designed for the adventurous spirit, focusing on the raw beauty of the island's interior before settling on the tranquil coast. The journey prioritises seclusion and immersion, offering a sophisticated alternative to the standard tourist circuit.

Unlike routes that rush through the greatest hits, this itinerary slows down in places that reward the patient traveller — riverside jungles, mist-covered ridges, a remote eastern sanctuary, and a stretch of coast that most visitors never find. It is Sri Lanka seen at its most honest and most beautiful.

01

Jungle & RiversideBelihul Oya

  • The expedition opens in the lush greenery of Belihul Oya, where riverside stays provide a peaceful introduction to the island's natural rhythms.
  • Swim in clear river pools beneath forest canopy, walk jungle trails, and wake to birdsong far from any tourist trail.
  • This off-the-beaten-track base is deliberately chosen to ease you into the island — body and mind — before the highland climbs begin.
02

Highland ExplorationHaputale

  • Travellers ascend to the dramatic ridges of Haputale — a narrow highland town perched on a knife-edge ridge with views dropping thousands of feet on both sides.
  • The focus here is physical immersion: expansive mountain walks and treks through mist-shrouded peaks at altitude.
  • Navigate challenging highland paths under the guidance of expert local guides, exploring hidden waterfalls and high-altitude landscapes that define the central hills.
  • Walk the colonial-era Lipton's Seat trail for a perspective over the entire southern tea country.
03

Highland TownElla

  • Drop down to the vibrant mountain town of Ella, buzzing with energy after the remote highlands.
  • Walk through tea estates to the famous Nine Arches Bridge — a colonial engineering triumph hidden in the jungle.
  • Catch a sunrise from Little Adam's Peak before the day warms and the valley comes alive with colour.
04

Eastern WildernessKumana National Park

  • After experiencing the energy of Ella, the journey shifts to the quiet east for an exclusive safari in Kumana National Park — one of Sri Lanka's most important and least-visited wildlife sanctuaries.
  • Kumana is famous for its exceptional birdlife (over 200 species including painted storks and spoonbills), as well as leopards, elephants, sloth bears and estuarine crocodiles.
  • Unlike the busier Yala, Kumana offers a far more secluded and intimate wildlife experience — fewer jeeps, more encounters.
05

Coastal SerenityTangalle

  • The route concludes at Tangalle's Silent Beach — a private and peaceful crescent of golden sand that offers total rest after the exertion of the highlands and safari.
  • Tangalle is one of the south coast's finest and least-commercialised beach towns, with calm bays, local restaurants and minimal crowds.
  • Seamless transfer back to Colombo Airport via the Southern Expressway, arriving rested and unhurried.
