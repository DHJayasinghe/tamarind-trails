B1 — Wildlife & Coast

The Wild Highlands
& East Coast Loop

Peak Performance, Coastal Calm

Duration 7 Days / 6 Nights Best For Ages 12–60 · Repeat Visitors · Nature Enthusiasts Best Months April – August Tiers Essential · Boutique · Signature
Route — Airport-Colombo → Belihul Oya → Haputale → Ella → Buduruwagala & Panama → Kumana NP → Tangalle → Airport-Colombo

A curated journey through Sri Lanka's most dramatic landscapes, designed for travellers seeking hidden gems over tourist crowds. The adventure opens in a pristine jungle riverside haven before ascending to the mist-shrouded peaks of Haputale's legendary tea country. The loop then swings east through the untouched wilderness of Kumana National Park, before concluding on the silent golden shores of Tangalle — a sophisticated alternative to the standard circuit, designed for those who already know the island and want to see it at its most honest.

The Journey

Six Chapters, Seven Days

01

Belihul Oya

Into the Mist

A riverside jungle hamlet where the island slows down — clear mountain pools, birdsong, and perpetual spring air to ease you into what lies ahead.

02

Haputale

Peak & Pekoe

Mist-shrouded ridges perched above seven provinces, Sir Thomas Lipton's legendary lookout, and some of the island's most dramatic highland terrain.

03

Ella

Hidden Valleys & High Vibes

Waterfall infinity pools, an iconic colonial railway bridge in the jungle, ancient caves, and a mountain town with a spirit that stays with you long after you leave.

04

Buduruwagala & Panama

The Deep East

A thousand-year-old rock-cut Buddha, a coastal lagoon teeming with wildlife, and a bonfire on a deserted beach — the Sri Lanka almost no visitor ever finds.

05

Kumana National Park

Wild Kumana

An exclusive, unhurried safari in one of the island's finest and least-visited sanctuaries — far fewer jeeps, and far more encounters.

06

Tangalle

The Slow Farewell

Golden sands, a final seafood dinner under the stars, and a seamless Southern Expressway transfer that ensures you leave the island rested and unhurried.

The full day-by-day itinerary — including stays, activities and pace — is shared exclusively upon enquiry.
