A — Heritage & Culture

Chronicles of Heritage
& Emerald Peaks

From the roots of ancient kingdoms to the heights of the misty hills

Duration 7 Days / 6 Nights Best For Ages 10–60 · Families · First-time Visitors Tiers Essential · Boutique · Signature
Route — Airport-Colombo → Anuradhapura → Habarana → Kandy → Nuwara Eliya → Ella → Galle → Colombo → Departure/Airport-Colombo

The journey through “Soulful Sri Lanka” begins in the Cultural Triangle, exploring the ancient wonders of Anuradhapura and Sigiriya before heading into the misty hills of Kandy and Nuwara Eliya. After a scenic train ride to Ella and a visit to the Nine Arches Bridge, the route concludes with the colonial charm of Galle Fort and a vibrant city tour in Colombo.

This itinerary is ideal for families and first-time visitors who want to experience the full sweep of Sri Lanka — from ancient civilisations and sacred temples to lush highlands, famous train journeys and a UNESCO-listed colonial fort — all at a thoughtful, unhurried pace.

01

Cultural TriangleAnuradhapura

  • Explore the sprawling ruins and towering stupas of the island's first capital — a testament to ancient spiritual and architectural grandeur.
  • Walk among the sacred Bodhi tree, the oldest documented tree in the world, and the immense Ruwanwelisaya Dagoba.
  • One of Asia's most significant archaeological sites, spanning over 40 square kilometres of sacred ground.
02

Cultural TriangleHabarana & Sigiriya

  • Immerse yourself in authentic village life in Habarana and witness wild elephant herds in their natural habitat at the edge of the lake.
  • Scale the legendary “Sigiriya Lion Rock” to witness 5th-century frescoes and world-class ancient urban planning set against a 360-degree jungle canopy.
  • These twin experiences — village community and ancient citadel — capture the living and historical heart of the island.
03

Hill CapitalKandy

  • Visit the sacred Temple of the Tooth Relic in this UNESCO-listed hill capital — the final stronghold of the ancient Kandyan kings.
  • Explore the Royal Botanical Gardens in nearby Peradeniya, home to over 4,000 plant species.
  • Experience a traditional Kandyan cultural show featuring drumming, dance and fire-walking.
04

HighlandsNuwara Eliya & the Scenic Train

  • Embark on one of the world's most iconic train journeys, winding through misty peaks and deep valleys into the heart of the highlands.
  • Explore the cool, colonial atmosphere of Nuwara Eliya — nicknamed “Little England” — surrounded by manicured tea estates.
  • Visit a working tea factory and taste single-estate Ceylon tea fresh from the fields.
05

HighlandsElla & Nine Arches Bridge

  • Marvel at the Nine Arches Bridge — a colonial-era engineering masterpiece hidden among lush tea bushes and tropical forests, best seen as a train passes overhead.
  • Hike up Little Adam's Peak for panoramic views across the surrounding valley and ridgelines.
  • Explore the vibrant café culture and market of Ella town in the evening.
06

Colonial SouthGalle Fort

  • Wander through the narrow cobblestone streets of the only “living fortress” in the country, where history and modern artisan boutiques collide.
  • Walk the ramparts of Galle Fort at sunset — a UNESCO World Heritage Site — watching the Indian Ocean turn gold below.
  • Browse independent boutiques, gem stores, and art galleries tucked within the fort's 17th-century walls.
07

City FinaleColombo

  • Conclude with a sensory tour of the capital, from the vibrant Red Mosque to high tea in the historic Dutch Hospital precinct.
  • Explore the colourful Pettah Market and Gangaramaya Temple before your departure.
  • A fitting finale — a city alive with the blend of cultures, cuisines and eras that define Sri Lanka.
